About BastilleBSD / Development Team

Development Team

The maintainers who steer the projects — and the contributors from around the world who build them.

BastilleBSD is open source through and through. A small group of maintainers guides the direction, reviews the code, and cuts the releases — but the projects are shaped by contributors across the FreeBSD community, from one-line fixes to whole subsystems.
