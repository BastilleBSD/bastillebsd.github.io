History

From a handful of personal shell scripts to a secure container toolkit used around the world.

Bastille has evolved over the years from a small collection of personal shell scripts into a secure container automation toolkit used on hardware ranging from the tiny Raspberry Pi to powerful servers in the data center and cloud.

The early scripts were informed by concepts from ezjail and iocage — both FreeBSD innovations in their time. Bastille would not exist without those that came before. Thank you.

Many of Bastille’s automation ideas come from lessons learned over a decade as a SaltStack contributor and the FreeBSD sysutils/py-salt package maintainer (now retired). Those concepts evolved into the Bastillefile template system, and then into our sister project, Rocinante, in 2021.

Combine those security and automation practices with FreeBSD’s production-tested foundation, and you have Bastille. Over the years, open-source contributors around the world have made it more robust, more mature, and better able to handle a wide range of use cases.
