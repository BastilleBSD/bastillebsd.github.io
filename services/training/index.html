Professional Services / Training Programs

Training Programs

Structured, instructor-led programs for mastering secure, production-grade FreeBSD and container management.

Secure infrastructure isn’t just about tools — it’s about the people who design, build, and maintain them.

Our training turns system administrators, DevOps engineers, and security professionals into confident practitioners of secure FreeBSD and container management. Programs combine expert-led instruction with hands-on labs so the skills actually stick.

The BastilleBSD Secure Systems Program

Our flagship program is an intensive, structured curriculum that progresses from FreeBSD fundamentals to advanced containerization and infrastructure automation.

  • Progressive curriculum — a clear path from foundational administration to advanced BastilleBSD and automation topics.
  • Hands-on labs — real-world scenarios that build confidence deploying and managing secure systems.
  • Self-assessments — regular checkpoints to reinforce and retain key concepts.
  • Learn from the source — instruction direct from the creator of BastilleBSD.

What you’ll master

Secure FreeBSD administration

  • Installation, configuration, and advanced tuning
  • Secure service deployment — SSH, pf, DNS, mail
  • ZFS storage management best practices
  • Patching strategy and OS lifecycle management

BastilleBSD containerization

  • Bastille’s architecture and security model
  • Jail provisioning and template automation
  • Advanced networking, NAT, firewalling, and virtual interfaces
  • Securing jail-to-jail and jail-to-host communication

Automation with Rocinante

  • Host bootstrapping and configuration management
  • Bastillefile templating for system-wide configuration
  • Reproducible environments across server fleets
  • Lightweight, GitOps-style automation for FreeBSD

Need these strategies implemented directly in your environment? Our consulting services pick up where training leaves off.

Who it’s for

  • System administrators deploying, managing, or migrating to FreeBSD

  • Security professionals hardening infrastructure
